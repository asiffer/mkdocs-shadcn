Thanks for considering contributing! 🎉

## Setup

Look at the [Setup page](./20_setup.md) or the [README](https://github.com/asiffer/mkdocs-shadcn#developers).

## Process

1. [Open an issue](https://github.com/asiffer/mkdocs-shadcn/issues) 🙏 So we can discuss about your bug or feature request. Use your own words, not AI.
2. Once we are ok, fork the project and write code.
3. Open a PR describing your changes. Use your own words.

!!! warning "Note:"
    Contributions are licensed under the [project's license](https://github.com/asiffer/mkdocs-shadcn/blob/master/LICENSE).

## AI

Do not use AI just to fill blanks with slop. 
You can use it in many cases without flooding the project:

- reviewing the code
- making the codebase as homogenous as possible
- creating translations
- writing tests
